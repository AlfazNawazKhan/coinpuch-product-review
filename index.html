Coinpuch Premium Tan Leather Document Folder

★★★★★
4.0 / 5 overall rating

I ordered this folder online and I'm happy with it. The warm tan finish looks premium and the stitching is neat. A full zip runs around the edges so my papers stay secure, and the inside is well organized: several card slots, a clear ID window, a zip pocket for important documents, a snap-closed pocket, and a ring binder section where my CV sits neatly in a clear sleeve. The handle on top is handy for carrying, but it's the one part I'd change. It's a good choice for students and job seekers who want to look professional. I'm giving it 4 stars instead of 5 mainly because of the handle.

What I liked

  • Warm tan finish with neat stitching
  • Full zip closure on three sides
  • Card slots, ID window and zip pocket
  • Ring binder keeps the CV in clear sleeves

What could be better

  • The handle should be made of something else instead of rubber
